Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What age does my child need to be to start Kindergarten?

Children entering KG 1 should be at least 4 years old by the start of the academic year in September. KG 2 is for children aged 5–6. If you are unsure which class is right for your child, our admissions team will be happy to advise you during your school visit.

Does Star Light accept mid-term transfers from other schools?

Yes. We welcome mid-term transfers subject to availability of places. Transfer students from JHS will sit a placement assessment to ensure they are placed in the correct class level. Please contact the school office directly for mid-term transfer enquiries.

What is the placement assessment for JHS applicants?

The placement assessment is a short, informal test covering Mathematics, English Language, and General Knowledge. It is designed to help us understand where your child is academically so we can support them effectively — it is not a pass/fail examination and should not cause any stress to your child.

How much are the school fees?

School fees vary by class level and are reviewed each academic year. Detailed fee information is provided to parents upon receipt of an offer letter. For a general fee enquiry, please contact our school office on +233 55 844 8280 or email admin@starlightinternational.edu.gh.

What subjects and extracurricular activities are available?

Core subjects include Mathematics, English, Science, Social Studies, Ghanaian Language, French, and ICT. We also offer STEM programmes, creative arts, sports, student leadership, public speaking, and cultural activities. Our goal is the total development of every child — academically, physically, and socially.

Is there a Parent Portal I can use to track my child's progress?

Yes. Star Light International School operates a full Parent Portal where enrolled families can view their child's academic results, attendance, fee status, announcements, and school communications in real time. Portal access codes are issued upon enrolment.
