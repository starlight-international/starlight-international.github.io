Term 3 Message · March 2026

Dear Parents,
Guardians & Students

Term 3, Academic Year 2025/2026

It is with great joy and pride that I welcome you to another edition of The Star Light Gazette. Term 2 is well underway and the energy and dedication I see across all our classes is truly inspiring.

Our students continue to demonstrate the values we hold dear at Star Light — discipline, hard work, respect and a genuine love of learning. As we approach our mid-term examinations, I encourage every student to stay focused, seek help when needed and give their very best.

To our parents and guardians, your partnership in your child's education remains invaluable. Thank you for the trust you place in us every day. Together, we are raising the next generation of Ghana's leaders.

May this term be one of growth, breakthrough and achievement for every child at Star Light.

The PrincipalStar Light International School
