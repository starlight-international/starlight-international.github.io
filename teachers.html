A Message from Our Principal

Building Character Through Knowledge

"Welcome to Star Light Islamic Basic School — a place where every child is valued, nurtured and given the very best opportunity to grow. Our school is built on three pillars: academic excellence, strong moral character and genuine love for every student who walks through our doors.

We believe that education is not only about passing examinations. It is about building human beings who are confident, compassionate, and ready to contribute to their families, communities and nation. Every member of our staff shares this vision and brings it to life in their classroom every single day."
